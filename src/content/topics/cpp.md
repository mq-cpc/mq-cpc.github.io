Supplementary, and not a step on the roadmap. These are C++ features worth
reaching for in a contest — things you can pick up and use straight away,
without needing to understand the rest of the language first.

The videos cover the containers and utilities that do the heavy lifting:
`vector`, `map` and `set`, iterators, `stringstream` for pulling a line apart,
and `getline` for line-based input, plus a longer look at string matching.

Treat it as a toolbox rather than a course. Take whatever is useful and leave
the intricacies of C++ until you actually run into them.
