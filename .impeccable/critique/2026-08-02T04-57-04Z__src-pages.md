## Design Specificity Verdict

Authored at the surface, generic at the structure.

The visual world could not be lifted onto another product. The hero terminal runs a real compile/run/submit session landing on the actual kattis-cli box-drawing banner. The brace mark, the heading prompt, the warm-paper ground and the two-temperature theme are committed, coherent and correctly tokenised.

The structure is interchangeable. Every page is eyebrow, h1, heading, card grid. `/tools` renders ONE card into a 3-col track, `/resources` ONE into a 2-col track, `/learn`'s Intermediate and Advanced bands ONE each into 3-col tracks. Reads as a broken build, not as honest thinness.

Biggest missed opportunity: roughly 25 club-recorded tutorial videos — the single most distinctive asset per PRODUCT.md — render as generic YouTube thumbnails. Unnumbered, untimed, no series framing.

Deterministic scan: 6 findings, all quality-category font/size advisories, zero structural slop. 3 of 6 are FALSE POSITIVES (SFMono-Regular, svg.css 15px, theme.css 17px root) — all from one mechanism: the detector parses the DESIGN.md typography YAML but not the prose rules where 15px, the 17px root, and the system-monospace exemption are documented.

REAL violations of the project's own Two-Label-Sizes Rule, in three places:

- `index.astro:77,78` — `font-size: 11px` (DESIGN.md says verbatim: do not introduce an 11/13/14px one-off)
- `graph-tool.css:46` — 13px on the label font, no exemption applies
- `index.astro:104,110,116` — three `text-[22px]` mono glyphs the detector cannot see, because a Tailwind arbitrary utility is not a `font-size:` declaration

## Overall Impression

A beautiful front door on a building with no reception desk. The hero does exactly the right emotional work — it shows the reward rather than the difficulty. Then the site has nowhere to put the visitor. It is designed as a content library that happens to meet in a room, while PRODUCT.md describes a club that happens to publish content.

## What's Working

1. The hero terminal is a real artefact, not a motif. The problem name is centred over the box-drawing width, and the table splits into a second `pre` at `line-height:1` because JetBrains Mono lacks U+2500. It works because it is accurate.
2. The token architecture delivers the two-temperature promise — every role swaps including accent hue and shadow tint. Dark passes contrast everywhere while light fails, so the light values are mis-tuned, not the system.
3. `/contribute` is a genuinely well-designed instructional surface — sequenced, names the literal GitHub UI strings, warns about the 404 before the user hits it, hides the CLI path behind a disclosure.

## Minor Observations

- The wordmark subtitle still credits ACM, which dropped ICPC sponsorship in 2018; it sits in the most-repeated element on the site, against Principle 2.
- No 404 page on a site positioned on permanence and addressability.
- `/contribute/website` links to an admitted work-in-progress stub.
- The heading prompt uses `0.7em`, so it renders 25px next to an h2 and 15px next to an h3 — one glyph reading as two marks.
- The card hover-lift is scoped to anchor cards, so the tools and events cards sit inert while visually identical cards elsewhere respond.
- resources.astro derives category hue from a char-code sum mod 6 — renaming recolours a category, and collisions are possible.
- 42 arbitrary-value Tailwind utilities, 20 of them in index.astro alone.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the job is converting a stranger into a member, why is the only conversion path a study curriculum? What if /events were the primary destination from the hero?
2. What is the design of the 25 videos? Framed as a numbered, timed course with a visible arc, does /learn become a curriculum spine instead of a card grid?
3. What does permanence look like as a visual property? Nothing expresses durability — no dates, no last-updated, no sense of a record accumulating.
4. Can honest thinness be designed rather than merely admitted? What would a section holding exactly one item look like if it were composed for one?
5. What is the smallest amount of state that would serve the returning member without auth? localStorage checkmarks need no backend and would resolve the largest failure for the co-primary user.
